## 01. Uncertainty Deliverable

1. Generate 3 additional examples of uncertainty. Make sure one of the examples represent different types of uncertainty.

Weather Forecast: The uncertainty about whether it will rain tomorrow represents natural variability, where atmospheric conditions are complex and not fully predictable.
Stock Market Performance: The uncertainty about how a stock will perform in the future involves financial and market-related factors that are subject to changes in investor sentiment and economic conditions.
Medical Diagnosis: When a doctor is uncertain about whether a patient has a particular disease due to overlapping symptoms, it represents diagnostic uncertainty, influenced by medical knowledge and the patient's individual health profile.


2. Please write a short summary of what the author means with the statement Probability does not exist.

The idea that the author was trying to convey is the subjectivity of probability and that the likelihood of something does not really exist. Rather, there is either that it will happen, 100%, or that it won't happen, 0%.


3. Please write a short summary of what it means to organize beliefs.

You should not only organize your beliefs but how those beliefs affect your course of action— what will you do based on what you believe. What is means to organize beliefs is to decide whether XYZ should be done, or not.

## Probability Likelihood Chance
1. In this case, the fact that team A has home field advantage does not affect the probability of any particular sequence of wins and losses. Each sequence still has the same probability of occurring, regardless of the order in which the games are played. Therefore, even with home field advantage, the series remains exchangeable. The probability of each individual outcome may be different due to the advantage, but the overall distribution of outcomes remains unchanged.

2. Team A has an advantage while playing in their own stadium, making them more likely to win those games. This introduces a bias into the outcomes, as the probability of Team A winning is not the same for every game. Consequently, the order of wins and losses becomes relevant. Certain sequences become more likely than others, depending on which team has home advantage in specific games. For example, the sequence "ABABA" is more likely than "BABAB".j Therefore, because the probability of an outcome changes based on the order of games and stadiums, the series would not be considered exchangeable when played alternating stadiums.

3. If the series is played either entirely in Stadium A or entirely in Stadium B, but the specific stadium is unknown, the series would be exchangeable. Exchangeability means that the order of outcomes does not impact the probability assessment of the series, and in this case, it would still hold true. When the specific stadium is unknown and there is an equal chance of the series being played in Stadium A or Stadium B, the probability of each team winning a game is the same for both stadiums. Therefore, the sequences of outcomes are exchangeable because the order of wins and losses does not impact the probability assessment. In this situation, the exchangeability is preserved due to the equal likelihood of either stadium hosting the entire series.

4. Probability: $P(\theta_i)$, likelihood: $P(\text{Series outcome is ABAAA} | \theta_i)$, chance: $P(\text{Series outcome is ABAAA})$
- The probability is the overall probability of an avent happening, in this case, the event is the eries being played in a certain stadium.
- The likelihood is the probability of observing a certain outcome given that we know something else about the situation. In this case, the outocme is the series outcome $\text{ABAAA}$ and the known information is that the series was played in stadium A.
- The chance is the probability of an event happening, regardless of any other information and the parameter in the prior probability which is $\theta_{i}$ given that the prior probability is $P(\theta_{i})$.
